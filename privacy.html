Who we are

Business Name ("we") is a pool cleaning business in Area Name, South Africa. We are responsible for the personal information you give us, as set out in the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Our Information Officer is Owner Name. Contact them at email@example.com or Phone Number.

What we collect

  • Your phone number and email address
  • The area you live in, and your address once you book
  • What you tell us about your pool, including any photos you send

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your quote request, book and do the work, and send you invoices. We never sell your information, and we don't send you marketing unless you ask for it.

How it reaches us

The quote form on this website does not store anything. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and you choose whether to send the message. WhatsApp is run by Meta, so its own privacy policy also applies to messages sent through it.

Google Maps

Our Get a Quote page shows a Google Maps map of where we are based. When it loads, Google may collect information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

Who we share it with

No one, except where the law requires it or where you ask us to (for example, a supplier we need for a repair).

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a customer, and after that only as long as we need it for our records or the law requires. Then we delete it.

How we keep it safe

Your details are kept on our phone and accounts, which are password protected, and only the people who do your job can see them.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see the information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can also tell us to stop contacting you. Just message us.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.
